# 005 Keycap Set, ⌘ ⌥ J

⌘⌥J opens the Console in Chrome DevTools on macOS. Three keycaps, one per key.

## Spec

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Artwork | `artwork/keycaps.svg` (blue dashed guides show the base and top outlines; do not print) |
| Material | PBT, 1.5 mm wall |
| Profile | Cherry |
| Stem | MX-compatible cross |
| Cap color | Black |
| Legend | PANTONE Cool Gray 6 C (`#a1a1a1`), reverse dye-sublimation (dye-sub can only darken, so the cap is dyed around a light legend) |
| Packaging | Three caps in a small black box with a printed ▼ (optional) |

| Key | Size | Row | Legend | Legend size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ⌘ | 1.25u (22.76 mm base) | R4 (bottom row) | Command, drawn as a 0.6 mm stroke | 5.4 mm |
| ⌥ | 1.25u (22.76 mm base) | R4 | Option, drawn as a 0.6 mm stroke | 5.6 mm wide |
| J | 1u (18 mm base) | R3 (home row) | J in Geist Mono Regular | 4.6 mm cap height |

Legends are centered on the top surface, 0.8 mm toward the back, like standard Cherry legends. J keeps the manufacturer's homing bar.

## Vendors

- **Sample and small runs:** Cherry-profile PBT dye-sub with no minimum from Goblintechkeys or Diykeycap (7 to 10 business days at Diykeycap).
- **Production:** dye-sub factories such as OSHID quote a 500-set minimum with 3 to 5 day sampling.
