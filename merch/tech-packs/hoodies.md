Fourthwall's embroidery limit for a standard area is 4 × 4 in and 15,000 stitches (help center, "Embroidery best practices"). The ▼ fits the area. It is a 5.4 sq in fill; at typical tatami density that is roughly 11,000 to 13,000 stitches with underlay and border, under the limit but not by much. Check the stitch count in the designer before publishing. If it is over, 3.25 in brings the area down by 14%; printing it is the other fallback.

## Care

Machine wash cold, inside out. Tumble dry low or hang dry.
