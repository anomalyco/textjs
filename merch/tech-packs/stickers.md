# 008–012 Stickers

Five single stickers, each its own Fourthwall product.

## Blank

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Fourthwall product | Allcolor 957 Die-Cut Stickers, from $3.78 base, no minimum |
| Sizes | 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 in (the artwork's longest side) |
| Stock | White vinyl, matte protective laminate, eco-solvent inks |
| Use | Water resistant, indoor and outdoor |
| Cut | Follows the artwork's outline: every non-transparent pixel is part of the sticker |

The vinyl is white and there is no transparent background, so each sticker's white edge is drawn into its artwork (0.12 in). Upload the PNGs in `artwork/png/` and keep everything outside the shape fully transparent (a reviewer on Fourthwall's sticker sheet listing found that partly transparent pixels change the cut). Fourthwall also warns that small details may be cut as one shape, which is why the ⌘⌥J keys sit on one black base rather than as three separate keys.
