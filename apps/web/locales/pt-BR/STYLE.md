# Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)

- Brazilian Portuguese, not European. Address the reader as você in docs, UI and marketing; never tu or o senhor. Instructions use the imperative: “Execute `bun dev`”, “Abra o console”.
- Brazilian vocabulary: arquivo, pasta, tela, navegador, servidor, padrão (default), celular, time (team), deploy (fazer deploy). Not ficheiro, ecrã, utilizador.
- Headings and labels are noun phrases or infinitives without a period: “Instalação”, “Abrir o console”, “Agrupar rotas”. Sentence case: only the first word and proper names take a capital.
- Quotes are “…”, with ‘…’ inside. Browser interface labels go in “…” and match Chrome's Brazilian interface: “Preservar registro”, “Configurações”. The Console panel is “Console”.
- Decimal comma in prose, with a no-break space (U+00A0) before units: 0,163 ms, 70,6%, 66 ms, 21 kB. Thousands take a period (3.200 células, 12.000 quadros). Never touch version numbers (Bun 1.2+, Node.js 22+, 1.4.1), numbers in code, paths or URLs.
- Dates read 3 de novembro de 2026. Ranges use an en dash: 70–81%.
- Genders: o console, o quadro, a rota, o layout, o hook, o componente, o tema, o span, o app.
- Keep API names, components, hooks, file paths, CLI commands and keyboard shortcuts (⌘⌥J, Ctrl+Shift+J, Enter) exactly as written.
- In a list or table, keep items parallel: all noun phrases, or all full sentences.
- Avoid translationese and filler: prefer a direct verb to “realizar”, “efetuar” or “fazer uso de”; active voice to “é renderizado por”. No gerundismo (“vamos estar enviando”), “é importante notar que”, “vale ressaltar”, “simplesmente”, “facilmente”, “de forma simples”, “poderoso”, “sem esforço”, “sem atrito”, “Vamos lá”, rhetorical questions, dramatic travessões or “não só… mas também”.
- Marketing copy keeps the deadpan tone: the console-as-production-UI premise is stated straight, never explained or winked at.
- Names kept in English: the Enterprise plan, the Playground and the course Learn Text.js. The Learn section and nav are “Aprender”; Docs are “Documentação”.
- Merch print slogans stay in English: “YOU WOULDN’T RENDER A WEBSITE IN THE CONSOLE”.

## Focus pad

- The console pad's actions finish the sentence “Then click here and {action}.” Translate each action as the form that fits that slot. “Depois clique aqui e {action}.”: {action} é um imperativo com você que fecha a frase: “digite”, “pressione ↑ ↓”, “digite um prompt”.
- “type” is the verb (type on the keyboard), never a data type. “Custom size” is the size picker's option for entering your own columns and rows.
- Hint labels under the keycaps are one or two lowercase words: “voltar”, “detalhes”, “carrinho”.

## Captions

- Subtitle style: about 17 characters per second. Drop filler and repetition, keep every fact, number and code name.
- “See you there.” at the end of a chapter is “Até lá.” “Welcome back to Learn Text.js.” is “Que bom ver você de novo no Learn Text.js.”
