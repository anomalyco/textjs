# Spanish (es)

- Neutral international Spanish for readers in Spain and Latin America. Address the reader as tú in docs, UI and marketing; never usted, and never vosotros (use ustedes for a plural). Instructions use the imperative: «Ejecuta `bun dev`», «Abre la consola».
- Avoid regional words where a shared one exists: no ordenador/computadora (say equipo or navegador), no coger, no vale as filler.
- Headings and labels are noun phrases or infinitives without a period: «Instalación», «Abrir la consola», «Agrupar rutas». Sentence case: only the first word and proper names take a capital.
- Questions and exclamations open with ¿ and ¡.
- Quotes are «…», with “…” inside. Browser interface labels go in «…» and match Chrome's Spanish interface: «Conservar registro», «Configuración». The Console panel is «Consola».
- Decimal comma, and a no-break space (U+00A0) before units and the percent sign in prose, so a number never wraps away from its unit: 0,163 ms, 70,6 %, 66 ms, 21 kB. Four-digit numbers take no separator (3200 celdas); larger ones take a period (12.000 fotogramas). Never touch version numbers (Bun 1.2+, Node.js 22+, 1.4.1), numbers in code, paths or URLs.
- Dates read 3 de noviembre de 2026. Ranges use an en dash: 70–81 %.
- Genders: la consola, el fotograma, la ruta, el layout, el hook, el componente, el tema, el span, la app.
- Keep API names, components, hooks, file paths, CLI commands and keyboard shortcuts (⌘⌥J, Ctrl+Shift+J, Enter) exactly as written.
- In a list or table, keep items parallel: all noun phrases, or all full sentences.
- Avoid translationese and filler: prefer «se renderiza» to «es renderizado», and a direct verb to «realizar», «llevar a cabo» or «proceder a». No «cabe destacar», «es importante tener en cuenta», «simplemente», «fácilmente», «de forma sencilla», «potente», «sin fisuras», «sin esfuerzo», «Vamos a…», the gerundio de posterioridad, rhetorical questions, dramatic rayas or «no solo… sino también».
- Marketing copy keeps the deadpan tone: the console-as-production-UI premise is stated straight, never explained or winked at.
- Names kept in English: the Enterprise plan, the Playground and the course Learn Text.js. The Learn section and nav are «Aprender»; Docs are «Documentación».
- Merch print slogans stay in English: «YOU WOULDN’T RENDER A WEBSITE IN THE CONSOLE».

## Captions

- Subtitle style: about 17 characters per second. Drop filler and repetition, keep every fact, number and code name.
- “See you there.” at the end of a chapter is «Nos vemos allí.» “Welcome back to Learn Text.js.” is «Te damos la bienvenida de nuevo a Learn Text.js.»
