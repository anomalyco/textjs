# Simplified Chinese (zh-CN)

- Mainland usage. Address the reader as 你, never 您. Instructions are plain imperatives: 运行 `bun dev`，打开控制台。
- Headings and labels are short noun or verb phrases without 。: 安装、打开控制台、路由分组.
- Full-width punctuation in Chinese text: ，。：；！？（）、 and “” with ‘’ inside. Keep ASCII punctuation inside code, paths, URLs and English names. A sentence that introduces a code block ends with ：.
- Put one half-width space between Chinese and Latin letters, digits or inline code: 使用 Text.js 构建、共 10 章、`app/` 目录、66 ms. No space next to full-width punctuation: （`app/`）、“设置”。No space between a number and % or °: 70.6%.
- Decimal point, no thousands separator under five digits: 0.163 ms、3200 个单元格、12,000 帧. Never touch version numbers, numbers in code, paths or URLs.
- Dates read 2026 年 11 月 3 日. Ranges use ～ or –: 70%～81%.
- Browser interface labels go in “” and match Chrome's Simplified Chinese interface: “保留日志”、“设置”. The Console panel is 控制台.
- Mainland terms: 代码、组件、文件、文件夹、目录、服务器、默认、运行、支持、函数、变量、对象、包、终端、窗口、设置、返回、渲染、布局、路由、帧、快捷键. Hook and props stay in English (Hook、props).
- Keep API names, components, hooks, file paths, CLI commands and keyboard shortcuts (⌘⌥J, Ctrl+Shift+J, Enter) exactly as written.
- In a list or table, keep items parallel: all noun phrases, or all full sentences.
- Avoid translationese and filler: write 渲染 not 进行渲染, drop needless 被 passives, 的 chains and pronouns (它、我们). No 值得注意的是、需要注意的是 as openers, 非常、轻松、简单地、无缝、强大、一站式、赋能、助力、让我们、rhetorical questions, ——, or 不仅……而且.
- Marketing copy keeps the deadpan tone: the console-as-production-UI premise is stated straight, never explained or winked at.
- Names kept in English: the Enterprise plan, the Playground and the course Learn Text.js. The Learn section and nav are 学习; Docs are 文档.
- Merch print slogans stay in English, in “”: “YOU WOULDN’T RENDER A WEBSITE IN THE CONSOLE”.

## Focus pad

- The console pad's actions finish the sentence “Then click here and {action}.” Translate each action as the form that fits that slot. “然后点击这里并{action}。”：{action} is a verb phrase that ends the sentence: 输入、按 ↑ ↓、输入提示词.
- “type” is the verb (type on the keyboard), never a data type. “Custom size” is the size picker's option for entering your own columns and rows.
- Hint labels under the keycaps are two to four characters: 返回、详情、购物车.

## Captions

- Subtitle style: about 7 Chinese characters per second. Drop filler and repetition, keep every fact, number and code name.
- Questions end with ？.
- “See you there.” at the end of a chapter is 下一章见。 “Welcome back to Learn Text.js.” is 欢迎回到 Learn Text.js。
