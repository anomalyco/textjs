# Traditional Chinese (zh-TW)

- Taiwan usage and vocabulary, not Hong Kong and not converted Simplified Chinese. Address the reader as 你, never 您. Instructions are plain imperatives: 執行 `bun dev`，開啟主控台。
- Headings and labels are short noun or verb phrases without 。: 安裝、開啟主控台、將路由分組.
- Full-width punctuation in Chinese text: ，。：；！？（）、 and 「」 with 『』 inside (not “”). Keep ASCII punctuation inside code, paths, URLs and English names. A sentence that introduces a code block ends with ：.
- Put one half-width space between Chinese and Latin letters, digits or inline code: 使用 Text.js 建構、共 10 章、`app/` 目錄、66 ms. No space next to full-width punctuation: （`app/`）、「設定」。No space between a number and %: 70.6%.
- Decimal point, no thousands separator under five digits: 0.163 ms、3200 個儲存格、12,000 個影格. Never touch version numbers, numbers in code, paths or URLs.
- Dates read 2026 年 11 月 3 日. Ranges use ～ or –: 70%～81%.
- Browser interface labels go in 「」 and match Chrome's Traditional Chinese interface: 「保留記錄」、「設定」. The Console panel is 主控台.
- Taiwan terms: 程式碼、元件、檔案、資料夾、目錄、伺服器、預設、執行、支援、函式、變數、物件、套件、終端機、視窗、設定、回傳、渲染、版面配置、路由、影格、快速鍵、網址、資訊、品質、使用者. Never the mainland forms (代码、组件、文件 for file, 默认、服务器、函数、对象、视频、信息、质量、用户). Hook and props stay in English.
- Keep API names, components, hooks, file paths, CLI commands and keyboard shortcuts (⌘⌥J, Ctrl+Shift+J, Enter) exactly as written.
- In a list or table, keep items parallel: all noun phrases, or all full sentences.
- Avoid translationese and filler: write 渲染 not 進行渲染, drop needless 被 passives, 的 chains and pronouns (它、我們). No 值得注意的是、需要注意的是 as openers, 非常、輕鬆、簡單地、無縫、強大、一站式、賦能、讓我們、rhetorical questions, ——, or 不僅……而且.
- Marketing copy keeps the deadpan tone: the console-as-production-UI premise is stated straight, never explained or winked at.
- Names kept in English: the Enterprise plan, the Playground and the course Learn Text.js. The Learn section and nav are 學習; Docs are 文件.
- Merch print slogans stay in English, in 「」: 「YOU WOULDN’T RENDER A WEBSITE IN THE CONSOLE」.

## Captions

- Subtitle style: about 7 Chinese characters per second. Drop filler and repetition, keep every fact, number and code name.
- Questions end with ？.
- “See you there.” at the end of a chapter is 下一章見。 “Welcome back to Learn Text.js.” is 歡迎回到 Learn Text.js。
