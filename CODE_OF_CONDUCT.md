# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We want everyone who takes part in Text.js to feel welcome, whatever their background or level of experience. Be respectful, accept feedback gracefully and focus on what's best for the project and the people using it. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing other people's private information are not tolerated.

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to [conduct@textjs.org](mailto:conduct@textjs.org). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions, or ban contributors, who break this code.
